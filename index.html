💰 Freelance Pro

Time tracking + Invoicing for freelancers

📖 Getting Started Guide - Complete Instructions ▼

🚀 Quick Start - 30 Seconds

1️⃣ Go to Quick Invoice tab → Enter client name → Add item → Generate PDF

2️⃣ OR go to Time Tracker tab → Start timer → Stop → Create invoice from tracked hours

⏱️ Time Tracker - Complete Guide

1Go to 👥 Clients tab → Click + Add Client
2Enter client name, email, and hourly rate → Click Save Client
3Go to ⏱️ Time Tracker tab
4Select project/client from dropdown
5Type what you're working on → Click ▶️ Start
6When done → Click ⏸️ Stop → Time is saved automatically
7Go to 📄 Invoices tab → Create Invoice from Time Entries
8Select client → Check time entries → Generate Invoice → Download PDF

⚡ Quick Invoice - Complete Guide

1Go to ⚡ Quick Invoice tab
2Fill Client Details (Name is required)
3Set Invoice Details (Number, Issue Date, Due Date)
4Add Items: Click + Add Item → Enter description, quantity, price
5Select Currency from dropdown (12 currencies available)
6Add Tax Rate (%) or Discount if needed
7Add Notes/Payment Terms (e.g., "Due in 30 days")
8Click 👁️ Preview to review invoice
9Click 📄 Generate & Download PDF → Save or email to client
💡 Pro Tip: Click "Save to history" when prompted to keep invoice in your records!

🌍 Language & Currency Guide

🌐Change Language: Top-right corner dropdown → Select your preferred language
💰Available Languages: English, Español, Français, Deutsch, हिंदी, العربية, 中文, Русский
💱Change Currency: Quick Invoice tab → Additional Information → Currency dropdown
🌎Available Currencies: USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), INR (₹), CNY (¥), SAR (﷼), KWD (د.ك), RUB (₽), BDT (৳), JPY (¥), CAD (C$), AUD (A$)

📊 Managing Your Data

📄View Invoices: Go to Invoices tab → See all your invoices
⬇️Download Invoice PDF: Click 📄 PDF button next to any invoice
✅Mark as Paid: In Invoices tab → Click "Mark Paid" when client pays
📤Export Backup: Click 📤 Export Data button in header → Saves all your data
📥Import Backup: Click 📥 Import Data button → Select your backup file
🗑️Delete Items: Click red Delete button next to any client, time entry, or invoice

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔹 Is my data safe?
✅ Yes! All data is stored locally in your browser. We never see or store your data on any server. Use the Export button to create backups of your data.
🔹 Can I use this on my phone?
✅ Yes! The app is fully responsive and works on mobile phones, tablets, and computers. The layout automatically adjusts to your screen size.
🔹 Do I need an account?
✅ No! You can use the app immediately without signing up. All data saves automatically to your browser's local storage.
🔹 What if I clear my browser cache?
⚠️ Your data will be lost! Always use the Export button to backup your data regularly. You can then Import the backup file to restore your data.
🔹 Can I change currency for existing invoices?
✅ Currency is set when creating the invoice. Existing invoices keep their original currency. Create a new invoice for different currencies.
🔹 How do I add multiple items to one invoice?
✅ Click "+ Add Item" button in the Quick Invoice tab as many times as needed. Each item appears as a separate row in the invoice.
🔹 Is there a limit on how many invoices I can create?
✅ No! You can create unlimited invoices, clients, and time entries. All data is stored locally in your browser.
💡 Need Help? Try these steps first:
• Refresh the page (Ctrl + F5 for hard refresh)
• Check if you filled all required fields (marked with *)
• Export your data as backup before making major changes
• Clear your browser cache if the app is behaving strangely
• Contact support: hello@freelancepro.com
